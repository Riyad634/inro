Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Engineering & Installation

Designing and deploying solar panel arrays on residential roofs, commercial buildings, and massive utility-scale solar farms. This service includes setting up net-metering systems to feed excess electricity back into the public grid.

Wind Energy Development & Maintenance

• Installing and maintaining onshore and offshore wind turbines. Wind energy companies handle everything from micro-turbines for remote communities to massive industrial turbine fields that generate gigawatts of electricity.

Hydroelectric & Hydrokinetic Power Solutions

Setting up large-scale dam generators as well as modern, eco-friendly "run-of-river" micro-hydro systems. These systems capture the natural flow of rivers and streams to generate continuous electricity without heavily disrupting local ecosystems

Biomass & Waste-to-Energy (WtE) Systems

Constructing facilities that convert organic waste, agricultural residues, and municipal solid garbage into electricity through controlled thermal processes or anaerobic digestion (biogas). This simultaneously solves waste management issues while generating power.
